Thunderstorm risk.

Stop play. Get inside a building or a hard-topped vehicle. Put the clubs down — they are metal.

Not safe: golf carts, course shelters, trees, open ground, water, metal fences.

Wait 30 minutes from the last thunder.

Any new thunder or lightning restarts the 30 minutes. Standard school policy is to suspend play when lightning is within 10 miles and to wait a full 30 minutes after the last one.

Distance to green

Not ready yet

How far you are from the front, center and back of the green on every hole, with a map showing where you are standing.

  • Works with no signal — GPS comes from satellites, not the network.
  • Your location stays on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Distance only, so it stays legal for tournament play.

This switches on once the home course has been measured, hole by hole. Until then it shows no yardages at all — a number that is wrong is worse than no number, because you would club off it.

Course not mapped yet. The layout below is an example course — a real one, standing in so the map and the distances can be seen working on real holes. The marker is an example ball, not where you are. Walk your own course with the mapping tool, then paste the result into data/course.json.
